iPhone Setup
  1. Tap Download above, then tap Share and pick "Save to Files."
  2. Open the Files app, touch and hold the file → Share → View More → "Use as Ringtone."
  3. Clock app → your alarm → Sound → it's at the top → Save.
iPhone on iOS 25 or older? Tap here
  1. Save the file to Files, and get the free GarageBand app.
  2. Press and hold the GarageBand icon on your Home Screen → "Create New Audio Recording."
  3. Tap the Tracks button (top), then the loop icon (top right) → Files → find your file.
  4. Touch and hold it, drag it all the way left onto the track.
  5. Tap the triangle (top left) → My Songs. Touch and hold the song → Share → Ringtone → Export.
  6. Clock app → your alarm → Sound → pick it → Save.
Android Setup
  1. Tap Download above.
  2. Clock app → your alarm → tap the sound → "Add new" (Google) or choose from device (Samsung).
